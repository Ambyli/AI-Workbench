# ai/oauth2-proxy

The Google sign-in for the browser UIs: Google login + Workspace-group check (`zeoai.access@zeoenergy.com`), one oauth2-proxy instance per public hostname. `oauth2-proxy-openwebui` serves `chat.zeoenergy.com` and routes by path to Open WebUI and the other browser UIs; `oauth2-proxy-superset` serves Superset at the root of `SUPERSET_PUBLIC_HOST` (`superset.zeoenergy.com`). Guide: [`OAUTH2_PROXY.md`](OAUTH2_PROXY.md). Compose: `docker-compose.oauth2-proxy.yml` (`oauth2-proxy-openwebui`, `oauth2-proxy-superset`, `oauth2-assets` — the nginx sidecar serving the repo's `assets/` for both instances' pre-auth pages). Config is the `OAUTH2_PROXY_*` block of `.env` (commented in `.env.example`); `oauth2-proxy-superset` shares most of it and fixes its differences in the compose file. Branded pages in `templates/`.

## Rules

- **Traffic path:** cloudflared → `oauth2-proxy-openwebui:4180` / `oauth2-proxy-superset:4180` (Docker DNS, never through the edge) → upstreams on `ai_shared`. LAN clients reach chat on host port 4180 through the edge (`listen oauth2-proxy-openwebui`); `oauth2-proxy-superset` has no LAN port. Grant / revoke access in Google Admin → Groups; revocation lands on the next `OAUTH2_PROXY_COOKIE_REFRESH` (15 m, hard expiry `OAUTH2_PROXY_COOKIE_EXPIRE` 1 h), on both hostnames.
- **Name each instance after the service it fronts:** `oauth2-proxy-<service>`, never the bare service name — on `ai_shared` that would shadow the real service (`superset:8088` would resolve to the proxy). `unit-tests/deploy` fails when two services answer to one name. `oauth2-proxy-openwebui` keeps the network alias `oauth2-proxy` only until the Cloudflare `chat` rule targets `oauth2-proxy-openwebui:4180`; then delete it and `make up oauth2-proxy` (`OAUTH2_PROXY.md § Renaming / cutover`).
- **`OAUTH2_PROXY_UPSTREAMS` (chat) is longest-prefix, path forwarded unchanged.** `.env.example` today: `openwebui:8080` (default), `/assets/` (`oauth2-assets`), `/sandboxes/` (`sandbox-proxy`), `/rustfs/` (`lake-s3:9001` console — prefix fixed by RustFS), `/edge/` (`edge:8407` status page). Apply with `make up oauth2-proxy`. A new browser UI goes under `chat.zeoenergy.com/<path>/` here rather than its own hostname, so it inherits the Google cookie (`ai/cloudflared/CLOUDFLARED.md § Current ingress rules`) — **unless it cannot run under a path prefix.** oauth2-proxy routes upstreams by path only (no Host matching), so such a UI gets its own hostname and its own instance. Superset is that exception: 4.0.2 loads `/static/…`, calls `/api/v1/…` and redirects to `/login/`, all of which chat hands to Open WebUI (`OAUTH2_PROXY.md § Superset on its own hostname`).
- **`oauth2-proxy-superset`** shares with chat, through `.env`: the Google client, cookie secret / expire / refresh, e-mail domains, group, admin e-mail, service account, templates and skip-auth routes. Fixed in its compose block: upstreams `http://superset:8088/` + `http://oauth2-assets:80/assets/`, cookie name `_oauth2_proxy_superset`, SameSite `lax`, `UPSTREAM_TIMEOUT` 60 s (Superset's own frontend timeout; the 30 s default would 502 a slow chart), `PASS_USER_HEADERS` `true`, `SET_XAUTHREQUEST` and `PASS_ACCESS_TOKEN` `false`. Swap-class with `stop_grace_period: 90s` (Superset's 60 s frontend timeout plus margin) and no edge listener. Bump its image tag with chat's.
- **Upstreams trust headers, with no password.** `PASS_USER_HEADERS` sets `X-Forwarded-Email` / `-User` on the request after deleting every client copy, underscore spellings included; Open WebUI's trusted-header SSO and Superset's `AUTH_REMOTE_USER` both read `X-Forwarded-Email`. `PASS_ACCESS_TOKEN` (chat only) → the Google access token Open WebUI's patch `0002` verifies the identity with (`OPENWEBUI.md § Single sign-on`). `SET_XAUTHREQUEST` only adds `X-Auth-Request-*` to **responses** (nginx `auth_request` mode): no upstream receives them, and oauth2-proxy does not delete a client's own `X-Auth-Request-*`, so nothing may trust that family. A header-trusting upstream must be reachable only through these proxies: Open WebUI is bound to `127.0.0.1:8007`, Superset's LAN port strips the headers at the edge. Keep chat's `PASS_USER_HEADERS` and `PASS_ACCESS_TOKEN` `true`.
- **`OAUTH2_PROXY_SKIP_AUTH_ROUTES=^/assets/`** is the only public path on either hostname (the logo on the sign-in page). Widening it bypasses group gating — a security decision.
- **`OAUTH2_PROXY_COOKIE_SAMESITE=none`** (chat) is load-bearing: sandbox iframes inside Open WebUI load from an opaque origin and `lax` would drop the cookie there (iframe → sign-in page). The cookie stays `HttpOnly` + `Secure`. Superset's instance needs no iframe and uses `lax`.
- **`OAUTH2_PROXY_COOKIE_SECRET`** must be 32 bytes of URL-safe base64, or both proxies exit at start.
- **Cloudflare caches authenticated responses** by file extension on every hostname the dashboard bypass rule does not cover — `chat.zeoenergy.com` and `superset.zeoenergy.com` both need it. Check `cf-cache-status` before blaming oauth2-proxy (`ai/cloudflared/CLOUDFLARED.md` § Cache rule — bypass for the authenticated hostnames). Verify the gates with `curl -sfL https://chat.zeoenergy.com/oauth2/ping` and `curl -sfL https://superset.zeoenergy.com/oauth2/ping`.
- **The branded pages are shared:** both hostnames show `templates/sign_in.html` ("Sign in to Zeo AI Chat").
- **Never commit** `sa-key.json` or the `.env` secrets (`OAUTH2_PROXY.md § What NOT to commit`).

## Cross-folder rules

Also in the root `CLAUDE.md` § Cross-folder rules — read the named entry there for the full picture.

- **Paths under `chat.zeoenergy.com`** — each path upstream has a partner that must change with it: `/sandboxes/` ↔ `SANDBOX_PROXY_URL` (`ai/sandbox`); `/rustfs/` is RustFS's own; `/edge/` ↔ `frontend status` in `ai/edge/haproxy.cfg`. The tunnel's `chat` rule (`ai/cloudflared`) ↔ `oauth2-proxy-openwebui:4180`.
- **A UI that cannot run under a path prefix gets its own hostname and its own oauth2-proxy** — `oauth2-proxy-superset`: `SUPERSET_PUBLIC_HOST` ↔ the tunnel public hostname (`ai/cloudflared`) ↔ the Google OAuth client's redirect URI ↔ the cache-bypass rule ↔ Superset's `PROXY_FIX_CONFIG` (`ai/trino`).
- **n8n is not exposed** — no upstream here routes to it. Exposing it adds another instance on its own hostname, built like `oauth2-proxy-superset`, and the n8n URL variables (`ai/n8n`) must match it. (root `CLAUDE.md` § Cross-folder rules)
- **Superset trusts a header** — `oauth2-proxy-superset` reaches `superset:8088` directly; never point its upstream at the edge's 8016, whose listener deletes the identity headers. `SUPERSET_REMOTE_USER_HEADER` (`X-Forwarded-Email`, `ai/trino`) must be a header `PASS_USER_HEADERS` sets; a new one also needs a `del-header` in the edge.
- **Swap-class services publish through the edge** — all three services are swap-class: no `container_name`, no `ports:`. Host port 4180 (`PORT_OAUTH2_PROXY`) is the edge's `listen oauth2-proxy-openwebui` (900 s timeouts): renaming the service renames that section, and the running edge must have it (`make up edge`) before the service's first swap. `oauth2-proxy-openwebui`'s `stop_grace_period: 900s` lets long chats finish on the retiring copy. Root `CLAUDE.md` § Deploys (`make up`).
- **Timeout ladder** — 900 s here (edge listen and `oauth2-proxy-openwebui`'s grace period) matches Open WebUI's outermost limit; don't shorten one side alone.

## Env vars

All in the oauth2-proxy block of `.env.example`, with comments; the table is `OAUTH2_PROXY.md § Configuration reference`. The ones with traps are above (`_UPSTREAMS`, `_SKIP_AUTH_ROUTES`, `_PASS_USER_HEADERS`, `_SET_XAUTHREQUEST`, `_PASS_ACCESS_TOKEN`, `_COOKIE_SAMESITE`, `_COOKIE_SECRET`, `_COOKIE_REFRESH` / `_EXPIRE`). `OAUTH2_PROXY_HTTP_ADDRESS` must keep `0.0.0.0:4180` (cloudflared reaches both instances on `ai_shared`); `OAUTH2_PROXY_COOKIE_DOMAINS` / `_REDIRECT_URL` must match the Google OAuth client's redirect URI; `OAUTH2_PROXY_GOOGLE_ADMIN_EMAIL` must be a Super Admin the service account impersonates (`OAUTH2_PROXY.md § One-time Google Admin console setup`). `PORT_OAUTH2_PROXY` (`4180`) is in the PORT REGISTRY (root `CLAUDE.md` § Env vars and the PORT REGISTRY).

- `SUPERSET_PUBLIC_HOST` (`superset.zeoenergy.com`) — `oauth2-proxy-superset`'s hostname: its cookie domain, whitelist and redirect URL `https://<host>/oauth2/callback`. Must equal the Cloudflare tunnel public hostname pointing at `http://oauth2-proxy-superset:4180` and be in the Google client's redirect URIs. `OAUTH2_PROXY.md § Superset on its own hostname`.
- `OAUTH2_PROXY_N8N_*` — leftovers of the removed n8n instance; nothing reads them.
